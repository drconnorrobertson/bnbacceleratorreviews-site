Questions to Ask About an STR Acquisition Service Scope

Before choosing an STR acquisition service, compare the written scope with the work needed to close and launch your property. A clear agreement is more useful than assuming every service described online is included.

Define the search work

Ask how the buy box is set, which markets are considered, how properties are screened, and what evidence accompanies a recommendation. Clarify how rejected properties and changes to the criteria are communicated.

Clarify diligence responsibilities

Identify who reviews local rules, association restrictions, seller revenue, physical condition, financing, and insurance. Ask which professionals you must retain directly. A service can coordinate diligence without replacing licensed legal, tax, lending, or inspection advice.

Separate closing from launch

Confirm whether furnishings, design, manager selection, photography, vendor setup, and listing launch are included, optional, or provided by others. Identify actual costs and who approves spending. Closing on the property does not mean it is ready for guests.

Review fees and milestones

Obtain the fee schedule, payment triggers, expected deliverables, client obligations, and termination terms in writing. Ask how a failed transaction or prolonged search affects the engagement. Compare services using the same scope rather than headline pricing alone.

Action checklist

  • Confirm screening deliverables
  • Assign diligence roles
  • Separate acquisition and launch scope
  • Review fee triggers
  • Document client responsibilities

Does a done-for-you service eliminate all buyer decisions?

No. The buyer still needs to approve criteria, financing, the purchase, major spending, and the professionals or providers involved.
